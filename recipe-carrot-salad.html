QUICK SALAD

KIMCHI CARROT
SALAD.

Sweet, tangy and slightly spicy — fresh, crunchy and ready in minutes.

01. WHAT YOU NEED

5 SIMPLE INGREDIENTS

  • Carrots2
  • Mini Kimchi Drop1 pouch (25–30 g)
  • Vinegar2 tbsp
  • Olive oil1 tbsp
  • Sugar1 tsp

02. HOW TO MAKE

3 SIMPLE STEPS

1

SHAVE THE CARROTS

Peel the carrots, then thinly slice or shave them into ribbons.

2

MAKE THE DRESSING

Combine Mini Kimchi Drop, vinegar, olive oil and sugar.

Mix until the sugar is dissolved and the dressing is smooth.

3

TOSS & REST

Add the carrots and toss until evenly coated.

Rest for 10–15 minutes, then serve fresh. Keep any leftovers refrigerated.

READY TO ENJOY
